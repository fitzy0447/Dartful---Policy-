Questions, answered

Before your first throw

What is Dartful?

A local multiplayer darts app for a real dartboard. Twenty game modes, Dartful Party championships, player profiles and match history — and no pressure to be any good at it.

Does Dartful track darts statistics and match history?

Yes. Saved players keep a profile with their match history and stats — games played, wins, best checkout and results per mode — kept over time instead of wiped at the end of the night.

Can I track records against friends?

Yes. Head‑to‑head records between saved players, all‑time leaderboards and win streaks mean your rivalries finally have a scoreline instead of two conflicting memories.

What is Dartful Party?

A multi‑game championship for 2–8 players. Pick a Quick, Classic or Epic length, choose from 17 eligible competitive modes or let Surprise Me build a balanced lineup, tweak the rules, then play for championship points with live standings, final‑round scenarios, awards and a shareable result card. Co‑op games sit outside the championship and stay available on their own. Full details here.

Does Dartful Party save championship history?

Yes. Every party is saved with titles, podium finishes, total championship points, win streaks and the current defending champion. More on Dartful Party.

Can I see who has won the most Dartful Parties?

Yes. The all‑time leaderboard ranks everyone by titles won, with podiums and total points beside them, and marks whoever is defending. See how history and rivalries work.

Does Dartful work with a normal dartboard?

Yes — any standard board and any darts, including the bent ones. You tap your throws in; no electronic board, no camera, no calibration.

Is Dartful good for casual darts with friends?

That's the whole point. Two to eight players on one screen, the app handling every rule, and party games that don't care what your average is.

Does Dartful have party games?

Eight original ones, plus three co‑ops where the whole group plays as one team — alongside nine classics scored properly. See the full lineup.

How many can play?

Two to eight on one shared screen, across competitive, team and co‑op modes. Most modes work from two players up — a few, like Checkout Horse, need at least three. Eight is chaos. Eight is recommended.

Is it free?

Free to download with three games a day. Unlimited play and Dartful Party are $9.99 a year, with no ads or other purchases.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No account to start a game. Play is local and shared‑screen, and profiles, history and Party records live on the device — so it works fine in a shed with bad reception.

Is it good for families?

The games are family‑friendly, but darts are pointy — kids should be supervised, with age‑appropriate kit and a clear throwing area.

Are new modes coming?

Yes. New modes turn up in app updates — and the twenty listed here all work right now.
