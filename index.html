Questions, answered

Before your first throw

What is Dartful?

A darts app for a real dartboard. Twenty game modes, all the scoring and turn order handled, and no pressure to be any good at it.

Do I need a special board?

No. Any standard board and any darts, including the bent ones. You tap your throws in — no electronic board, no camera, no calibration.

How many can play?

Two to eight on one shared screen, across competitive, team and co‑op modes. One exception: Checkout Horse needs at least three players to work. Eight is chaos. Eight is recommended.

Is it free?

Free to download, with three games a day on the house. Every mode unlocked is $9.99 a year, auto‑renewing, cancel anytime. No ads and nothing else to buy.

Do I need an account?

No account to start a game. Play is local and shared‑screen, so it works fine in a shed with bad reception.

Is it just a scoring app?

It scores the classics properly — 501, Cricket, Shanghai — and adds eleven original party and co‑op games built around the board.

Is it good for families?

The games are family‑friendly, but darts are pointy — kids should be supervised, with age‑appropriate kit and a clear throwing area.

Are new modes coming?

Yes. New modes turn up in app updates — and the twenty listed here all work right now.

Who makes this

Hi, I'm Leigh

I'm a solo developer, and I'm resolutely average at darts. What I actually enjoy is playing with mates — and it's the arcade modes we keep coming back to, funnily enough.

I built Dartful because it's the app I wanted for our own nights — proper scoring for the classics, plus a pile of games built around a real board with people in the room. Couldn't find one that did both, so I built it myself.

If you've got a mode in mind, send it to me — dartfulapp@gmail.com. I'd rather keep the list tight than pile things on for the sake of it, but if it's a good idea, I'll find room for it. And somewhere down the track I'd like to ship a mode builder, so you can put together whatever game you can dream up.

Grab your darts. I'll keep score.
