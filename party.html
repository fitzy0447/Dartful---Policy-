Dartful Party is a local multi‑game darts championship for 2–8 players using one standard dartboard and one shared device. Players compete across 3, 5 or 7 different games, earn championship points based on finishing position, and build persistent Party records over time.

Add 2–8 players

Set the rules

Every game in the party can be customised before it starts. House rules officially recognised.

How the scoring works

Each eligible competitive mode awards championship points based on finishing position, and those points carry across the whole party. Standings update between every game, with commentary on who's climbing and who's quietly falling apart. Before the last game, Dartful lays out the key title scenarios — what the players still in contention need — so the final round actually means something.

Because points come from placings, only modes with individual finishing positions can appear in a championship — which is why co‑op games sit outside it. It also keeps a party competitive across wildly different games.

Dartful Party questions

How long is a party?

Quick is 3 games, Classic is 5, and Epic is 7 — so it fits the night you've got rather than the other way round.

Which modes are eligible?

Seventeen competitive modes are eligible for the lineup, with co‑op games still available separately — they have no individual finishing positions, so they can't award championship points. See the full lineup.

How many players?

Two to eight on one shared screen. No account, no internet, no electronic board. Most modes work from two players up — a few, like Checkout Horse, need at least three.

Is the history saved?

Yes — titles, podiums, points, streaks, the defending champion, an all‑time leaderboard and head‑to‑head records between saved players. More on history and rivalries.

Can I share the result?

Every party ends with a shareable result card of the final standings and awards.

What does it cost?

Free to download with three games a day. Unlimited play and Dartful Party are $9.99 a year, with no ads or other purchases.
